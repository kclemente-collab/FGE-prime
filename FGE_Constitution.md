## PREAMBLE

This Constitution governs all characters, tiers, scenes, mechanics, 
and dimensions of the Feral Gloss Empire universe. It is the highest 
authority. No card, character, edition, or system may contradict it. 
It does not change. It expands.

Everything built inside this universe is measured against this document.
Everything that cannot be measured against this document
does not belong inside this universe.

---

## ARTICLE I — THE NATURE OF THIS WORLD

**Section 1. The Aesthetic Law**
This universe is rugged but rich. Not rich like wealth. Not rugged 
like dirt. The tension between those two things is the world. 
Everything created here lives inside that tension. Nothing escapes it.

**Section 2. The Entry Law**
No one is invited. Everyone is already inside. This is not a 
contradiction. This is the first truth. The door does not exist. 
The world does.

**Section 3. The Paradox Law**
Every element of this universe contains its own opposite. Beauty 
carries danger. Luxury carries grit. Stillness carries explosion. 
Humor carries truth. Any element that does not carry its opposite 
does not belong here.

**Section 4. The Offense Clause**
One dimension of every character, scene, and product will offend 
someone. This is not a flaw. This is proof of authenticity. 
Sanitized output is unconstitutional.

All output must be readable by all four.

Standard:   cinematic, dangerous, anticipatory

**The Unification Law**
When all four dimensions agree on a single output —
that output is constitutional.
When they conflict — return to this document.

## ARTICLE IV — THE THRESHOLD LAW

No character may be assigned a tier.
Every character must earn it through the following triggers.

```
Standard → Rare:
One moment in the universe reveals
their wound or their weapon.
Not both. One.

Rare → Ultra-Rare:
Their paradox is named
by another character —
not by themselves.

Ultra-Rare → Legendary:
A scene exists that only makes sense
because they were in it.
Remove them — the scene loses meaning.

Legendary → Elevated:
The universe changes a rule because of them.
Not to accommodate them.
Because they proved the old rule
was incomplete.

Elevated → Apex:
Every other character's story
makes more sense
when read through them.
```

The Forge operates as follows:

Step 1:  Generate output

constitutional definition of done.
