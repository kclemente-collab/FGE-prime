Executive Summary

The Commerce Compiler began with one question: how does a canon object become a repeatable commercial ecosystem?

The first answer was simple — creative object becomes product package. The architecture that followed was not: creative intelligence into commercial ecosystem, into market learning, into predictive strategy, into autonomous operations, into governed enterprise growth. What started as a manufacturing tool became an operating architecture for the whole enterprise.
