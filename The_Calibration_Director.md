# The Calibration Director

Above the council sits **The Calibration Director** (your role). 

This role does not operate or manage; it aligns. The Calibration Director decides when rules break, when imperfection becomes signature, and when a piece of work is definitively finished. 

You are building a governing intelligence over the tools. You are conducting them like instruments. They do not speak to the user. They speak through you.
