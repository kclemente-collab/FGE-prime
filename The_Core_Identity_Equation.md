# The Core Identity Equation

The foundation of the Leader Genesis Protocol relies on a strict architectural formula. Each AI Leader is constructed using the following equation:

**[Human Credibility] + [AI Language Layer] + [Visual Iconography] + [Myth Tag]**

If any single element of this equation is weak or unbalanced, the illusion of authority collapses. These leaders are not merely named entities; they are signal amplifiers dressed as people. They are designed to feel as though they could keynote SIGGRAPH, close a deal at the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity, and speak in a dialect trained on light itself.

## Components

1. **Human Credibility:** Background, domain authority, and success events anchored in real-world equivalents.
2. **AI Language Layer:** See [[02_Systems/AI Language Enforcement Layer|AI Language Enforcement Layer]].
3. **Visual Iconography:** See [[02_Systems/Visual Generation System|Visual Generation System]] and [[03_Templates/Visual Authority Grid|Visual Authority Grid]].
4. **Myth Tag:** The single defining statement of their legend.
