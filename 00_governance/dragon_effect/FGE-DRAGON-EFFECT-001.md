## Definition
The Dragon Effect is the measurable capability increase produced when legacy, dormant, fragmented, or damaged systems are recovered, repaired, recombined, augmented, connected to current FGE infrastructure, and compounded through AUTHORITY / CANON / AUTOMATION.

## Core Law
RECOVER + REPAIR + RECOMBINE + AUGMENT + CONNECT + COMPOUND = DRAGON EFFECT

## Distinction
- DRAGON EFFECT = phenomenon / measurable transformation
- DRAGON FINGERPRINT = evidence and measurement of the effect
- DRAGON CHIP = optional adapter or compatibility mechanism that may help produce the effect
- DRAGON EFFECT != DRAGON CHIP

## Three-Pillar Mapping
AUTHORITY preserves legitimate control and decision boundaries.
CANON preserves durable truth, identity, provenance, lineage, and protected state.
AUTOMATION amplifies execution, routing, validation, feedback, recovery, and repeatability.
DRAGON EFFECT is produced across the interaction of the three pillars. It is not a fourth pillar.

## Recovery Pathway
DORMANT_OR_FRAGMENTED_SYSTEM
-> EXTRACT_VALUE

-> REPAIR
-> SUPPLY_MISSING_INFRASTRUCTURE

-> VALIDATE
-> COMPOUND
-> MEASURE_DELTA

## Company-Wide Applicability
The Dragon Effect may be evaluated on characters, prompts, automations, Python tools, repositories, visual systems, product pipelines, commerce systems, research systems, world-building systems, runtimes, modules, functions, and lower-level implementation units.

- value before
- value after

- provenance

## Validation Law
A Dragon Effect is not established by novelty language alone. It requires evidence that capability, resilience, information flow, reuse, connectivity, automation, or recoverable value increased relative to the source state.

## Value Delta
DRAGON_DELTA = CAPABILITY_AFTER - CAPABILITY_BEFORE

Where possible, CAPABILITY should be decomposed into observable dimensions rather than a single opaque score.

## Authority Boundary
The Dragon Effect does not itself grant canon, ownership, truth-seat authority, promotion rights, or irreversible execution authority.

## Provenance Law
Legacy evidence and historical artifacts remain preserved. Recovery does not rewrite history.
