## Definition
The Dragon Effect is FGE's company-level success metric and trophy-case system for proven work that creates durable value.

It records what demonstrably worked, what became dependable, what became reusable or modular, what created new creative capability, and what produced meaningful commercial or operational value.

It is NOT a deck of commandments, a scripture-like ruleset, or merely a lesson-learned archive.

It is a performance and value-recognition system.

## Trophy-Case Function
The Dragon Effect should preserve and surface proven successes such as:
- completed creative works and flagship IP, including Gray Day novel-class achievements when evidenced
- successful and dependable infrastructure
- reliable automation
- reusable creative modules
- repair systems that restore capability
- troubleshooting systems that reduce failure cost
- modular systems that compound into larger capability
- novelty that becomes useful rather than merely interesting
- systems that increase throughput, quality, reuse, resilience, or revenue potential

The trophy case is evidence-led. Inclusion requires receipts, outcomes, or other admissible proof.

## Core Question

## Success Metric
DRAGON_SCORE is not a moral score. It is a value-performance score.

A candidate Dragon Effect entry should answer:
- What succeeded?
- What proof exists?
- What value did it create?
- Was it dependable?
- Was it reusable?
- Did it reduce effort or failure?
- Did it unlock further systems?
- Did it create novelty that became useful?
- Did it create or support commercial value?

## Paid-On Interpretation
The Dragon Effect may serve as a reward, compensation, value-accounting, or performance-basis metric where the Director chooses to bind compensation or incentives to demonstrated system value.

No specific payment formula, compensation obligation, or payout authority is created by this object unless separately defined and authorized.

## Three-Pillar Mapping
AUTHORITY records who legitimately owns, approves, or claims the success.
CANON records the durable artifact, result, provenance, and verified achievement.
AUTOMATION records repeatability, leverage, throughput, resilience, and automated value.

DRAGON EFFECT measures the successful value produced across those pillars. It is not a fourth pillar.
