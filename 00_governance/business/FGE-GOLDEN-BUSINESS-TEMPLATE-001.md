## PURPOSE
This is a business evaluation template, not a systems architecture, doctrine, commandment set, or lesson-learned register.

It exists to give FGE one high-value frame for testing whether an idea, revived capability, creative work, product concept, modular tool, infrastructure improvement, or commercial opportunity has enough substance to stick somewhere in the company.

Use it as a bounce surface:
IDEA -> PLACE -> VALUE -> PROOF -> LEVERAGE -> PAYOFF POTENTIAL.

## GOLDEN QUESTION
If this works, where does it create durable business value, why would FGE keep it, and what evidence would prove that it deserves to stay, scale, combine, or earn recognition?

## CORE BUSINESS FRAME
Every candidate is evaluated across six business dimensions.

### 1. VALUE SOURCE
What valuable thing is present?
- original creative value
- recovered legacy value
- repaired capability
- reusable component
- product value
- customer value
- operational value
- commercial value
- knowledge value
- brand/world value

### 2. THREE-PILLAR LANDING
Where does the value land?

AUTHORITY
- Does this clarify ownership, decision rights, accountability, approval, delegation, or escalation?

CANON
- Does this preserve or improve durable identity, truth, provenance, continuity, reusable knowledge, product memory, or business state?

AUTOMATION
- Does this reduce repeated work, create leverage, improve reliability, enable scale, add routing, validation, recovery, feedback, or autonomous execution?

A candidate may land in one pillar, two pillars, or all three.

### 3. DRAGON EFFECT
Does the candidate recover, repair, recombine, augment, connect, or compound existing value?

DRAGON EFFECT CHECK:
- legacy value recovered
- provenance preserved
- broken edges repaired
- missing infrastructure supplied
- old and new capability connected
- automation increased
- resilience increased
- information flow improved
- novelty emerged
- reuse/compounding increased

The Dragon Effect is a success effect, not a commandment.

### 4. CREATIVE VALUE
Does the candidate generate something worth caring about?

Assess:
- originality
- expressive strength
- narrative/world contribution
- surprise
- aesthetic or conceptual distinction
- modular creativity
- recombination potential
- audience/product potential
- ability to generate further ideas

Creative value may be proven by works such as Gray Day or by reusable creative frameworks that repeatedly produce strong outcomes.

### 5. BUSINESS LEVERAGE
What changes if this succeeds?

Assess:
- workload reduced
- throughput increased
- quality improved
- reliability improved
- repair cost reduced
- reuse increased
- time-to-output reduced
- new product surface created
- commercial optionality increased
- downstream systems unlocked
- future ideas become easier to execute

### 6. PROOF / TROPHY / SCORE
What would make this count?

Evidence may include:
- shipped creative work
- dependable infrastructure
- successful automation
- repeated reuse
- successful repair/revival
- measurable time savings
- quality gains
- commercial result
- adoption
- downstream unlocks
- test receipts
- user/director acceptance

The Dragon Score is the scoring/recognition layer for proven value. The Trophy Case is the recognition surface for things that demonstrably worked.

## QUICK BOUNCE TEST
Use this for rapid idea screening.

1. What value is actually here?
2. Which pillar receives it: AUTHORITY, CANON, AUTOMATION?
3. Does it recover or compound anything we already own?
4. Does it create creative, operational, or commercial leverage?
5. What breaks if we ignore it?
6. What must be true for it to work?
7. What proof would make us keep it?
8. Can it become reusable or modular?
9. Can it reduce future work or open future products?
10. If successful, would it deserve a Dragon Score or Trophy Case entry?

If the idea cannot answer these questions yet, preserve it as a candidate rather than forcing a home.

## BUSINESS FIT MATRIX

| Dimension | Weak | Useful | Strong | Golden |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business value | vague | local gain | material gain | strategic/compounding gain |
| Pillar fit | unclear | one route | multi-route | tri-pillar reinforcement |
| Dragon Effect | none | repair/reuse | recovery + connection | compounding transformation |
| Creative value | generic | useful variation | distinctive | generative / world-building / product-defining |
| Reliability | fragile | works once | repeatable | dependable infrastructure |
| Modularity | isolated | reusable part | composable | multiplies other systems |
| Automation | manual | assisted | automated | self-improving/feedback-enabled |
| Commercial value | none/unknown | indirect | plausible | proven or high-leverage |
| Proof | opinion | example | repeated evidence | receipts + outcomes + adoption |

## GOLDEN SIGNAL
A candidate approaches Golden status when it does several of these at once:
- creates real value
- has a clear company landing
- strengthens one or more pillars
- preserves/reuses prior investment
- is dependable
- is modular
- reduces future work
- generates further creative or commercial possibility
- has receipts
- compounds rather than merely completes

## RELATIONSHIPS
FGE-GOLDEN-BUSINESS-TEMPLATE-001 uses:
- AUTHORITY / CANON / AUTOMATION as company coordinates
- Dragon Effect as a compounding-success lens
- Dragon Score as a future/proven value scoring layer
- Trophy Case as recognition of demonstrated success
- reverse-engineering / legacy extraction as a source of recoverable business value
- creative frameworks as business-value generators, not technical architecture by default

## BOUNDARY
This template evaluates business fit and business value. It does not itself:
- grant authority
- promote canon
- execute automation
- assign compensation
- declare a Dragon Score without evidence
- force every idea into an existing system

Unknown > invented. Evidence > enthusiasm. A strong idea without a home remains a candidate until a real landing is found.
