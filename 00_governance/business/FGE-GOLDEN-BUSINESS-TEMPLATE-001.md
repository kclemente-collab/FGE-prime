## PURPOSE
This is FGE's business judgment template. It is not a systems architecture, doctrine deck, commandment set, technical framework, or lesson-learned register.

It is the gold-standard lens through which FGE evaluates canon brainstorming, automation brainstorming, creative concepts, revived legacy systems, products, infrastructure, characters, tools, research findings, and commercial opportunities before investing further effort.

Use it as a bounce surface:
IDEA -> LANDING -> VALUE -> PROOF -> LEVERAGE -> COMPOUNDING -> BUSINESS DECISION.

The template is designed to improve over time as FGE accumulates real examples, receipts, failures, successful infrastructure, creative wins, sales, and Dragon Effect evidence.

## GOLDEN QUESTION
If this works, where does it create durable business value, why would FGE keep it, what existing value does it recover or amplify, and what evidence would prove that it deserves to stay, scale, combine, sell, or earn recognition?

## COMPANY COORDINATE SYSTEM
Every durable FGE object can be located through three company coordinates:

AUTHORITY
Who may decide, approve, mutate, promote, delegate, or stop it?

CANON
What is durable, governing, identity-bearing, historical, versioned, or truth-bearing about it?

AUTOMATION
What can execute, repeat, route, react, validate, recover, compile, or operate without repeated manual work?

These are company coordinates, not departments.

A candidate may land in one pillar, multiple pillars, or at an explicit intersection.

AUTHORITY x CANON = GOVERNANCE
AUTHORITY x AUTOMATION = PERMISSIONED EXECUTION
CANON x AUTOMATION = STATEFUL RUNTIME
AUTHORITY x CANON x AUTOMATION = GOVERNED COMPANY OPERATING SYSTEM

Unmapped does not mean worthless. It means the business landing is not yet demonstrated.

## COMPANY-WIDE FILTER LAW
All substantial FGE brainstorming should be testable through this lens before expansion:

CANON BRAINSTORMING
Ask whether the proposed durable truth, identity, rule, object, or relationship creates enough continuity, reuse, product value, proof, or downstream leverage to justify preservation or promotion.

AUTOMATION BRAINSTORMING
Ask whether the proposed automation removes meaningful work, increases reliability, unlocks scale, repairs failure, improves routing, creates feedback, or materially expands execution capacity.

CREATIVE BRAINSTORMING
Ask whether the idea produces distinction, expression, story, audience value, product potential, reusable creative grammar, or further generative possibility.

BUSINESS BRAINSTORMING
Ask where the value lands, who benefits, what changes, how it can be proven, and whether successful execution compounds.

The filter does not kill ideas that lack proof. It keeps them in the correct state until evidence arrives.

## THE REVERSE CHIP LENS
The Reverse Chip is the recovery question set applied to existing or dormant FGE work.

Do not begin with: How do we rebuild this old system?

Begin with:
- What capability is trapped inside it?
- What already worked?
- What remains uniquely valuable?
- What assumptions are obsolete?
- What is broken?
- What evidence or provenance survives?
- Which part maps to AUTHORITY?
- Which part maps to CANON?
- Which part maps to AUTOMATION?
- What adapter, repair, missing infrastructure, or translation would make the useful part viable now?
- Can the recovered capability plug into a newer method without forcing the old architecture to survive intact?

Reverse Chip output classes:
- RECOVER AS-IS
- REPAIR
- ADAPT
- HARVEST COMPONENTS
- RECOMBINE
- RESEARCH FIRST
- ARCHIVE ONLY

The Reverse Chip is a business recovery tool. It is valuable when it converts sunk creative or technical investment into current capability.

## DRAGON EFFECT
The Dragon Effect is the measurable success effect produced when value is recovered, repaired, recombined, augmented, connected, and compounded.

RECOVER + REPAIR + RECOMBINE + AUGMENT + CONNECT + COMPOUND = DRAGON EFFECT

A Dragon Effect claim should leave recognizable evidence:
1. legacy value recovered
2. provenance preserved
3. broken edges identified
4. missing infrastructure supplied
5. old and new capability connected
6. automation capability increased
7. failure resilience increased
8. information flow increased
9. novel capability emerged
10. reuse or compounding increased

The Dragon Effect is not doctrine. It is evidence that the company got stronger.

## DRAGON SCORE
The Dragon Score is the value-recognition layer applied after evidence exists.

It should evaluate component dimensions rather than hide everything inside one opaque number.

Candidate dimensions:
- creative success
- infrastructure dependability
- modular reuse
- automation leverage
- repair or recovery value
- troubleshooting value
- novelty value
- compounding value
- commercial value
- proof quality

DRAGON SCORE != enthusiasm.
DRAGON SCORE != canon authority.
DRAGON SCORE != compensation formula.

It may support future recognition or payout systems only where those systems are separately authorized.

## TROPHY CASE
The Trophy Case is the recognition surface for work that demonstrably succeeded.

Possible trophy classes include:
- CREATIVE WORK
- DEPENDABLE INFRASTRUCTURE
- MODULAR SYSTEM
- AUTOMATION WIN
- RECOVERY / REPAIR WIN
- COMMERCIAL WIN
- COMPOUNDING WIN

Gray Day is a Director-named example of creative-success trophy class.

Past winners are comparables, not commandments. They teach the lens what success can look like without forcing future work to imitate them.

## CREATIVE FRAMEWORK
FGE creative value is evaluated as business value when creativity produces one or more of the following:
- meaningful distinction
- stronger expression
- story or world expansion
- reusable grammar
- recombination potential
- audience interest
- product potential
- brand value
- new capability
- new automation opportunity
- further idea generation

Creative novelty alone is insufficient. The question is whether novelty creates leverage, meaning, demand, reuse, or a new productive direction.

## GOLDEN FIT MATRIX
| Dimension | Weak | Useful | Strong | Golden |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Business value | vague | local gain | material gain | strategic or compounding gain |
| Company landing | unclear | one route | multi-route | tri-pillar reinforcement plus product/business surface |
| Reverse Chip | none | isolated reuse | meaningful recovery | old investment becomes new capability |
| Dragon Effect | none | repair/reuse | recovery + connection | compounding transformation |
| Creative value | generic | useful variation | distinctive | generative, product-defining, or world-building |
| Reliability | fragile | works once | repeatable | dependable infrastructure |
| Modularity | isolated | reusable part | composable | multiplies other systems |
| Automation | manual | assisted | automated | feedback-enabled / resilient / compounding |
| Commercial value | none/unknown | indirect | plausible | proven or high-leverage |
| Proof | opinion | example | repeated evidence | receipts + outcomes + adoption |

## GOLDEN SIGNAL
A candidate approaches Golden status when it does several things at once:
- creates real value
- has a clear company landing
- strengthens one or more pillars
- recovers prior investment
- repairs a weakness
- is dependable
- is modular
- reduces future work
- generates further creative or commercial possibility
- can become productized or operationalized
- has receipts
- compounds rather than merely completes

## BOUNDARY
This template evaluates business fit and business value. It does not itself:
- grant authority
- promote canon
- execute automation
- assign compensation
- declare a Dragon Score without evidence
- convert legacy claims into present truth
- force every idea into an existing system

UNKNOWN > INVENTED.
EVIDENCE > ENTHUSIASM.
VALUE > COMPLEXITY.
COMPOUNDING > ACCUMULATION.

A strong idea without a home remains a candidate until a real landing is found.
