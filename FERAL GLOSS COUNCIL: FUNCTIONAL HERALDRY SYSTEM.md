# FERAL GLOSS COUNCIL: FUNCTIONAL HERALDRY SYSTEM

This document defines the **Functional Heraldry** for each leader of the Feral Gloss Council, anchoring their symbolic authority to concrete operational behaviors, inherent risks, and conditions for evolutionary adaptation. This system transforms them from static icons into dynamic, self-regulating behavioral engines.

---

## I. AURELIO VANTREX: THE SCOUT

**Signature Neurological Trait:** Predictive Signal Lock

### Functional Crest: The Fractured Compass

*   **Core Sigil:** A fractured compass, its needle perpetually pointing towards an unseen future horizon, surrounded by faint, overlapping signal rings.
*   **Execution Markers (Non-negotiable Outputs):**
    *   Produces a minimum of **3 validated signals** of future opportunity per operational cycle.
    *   Identifies **1-2 emerging patterns** from fragmented data that will be critical in the next 72 hours.
    *   Delivers actionable intelligence with **>90% predictive accuracy** on market shifts.
*   **Failure Scar (Critical Collapse Point):** Blindness to present details; an over-reliance on future prediction can lead to overlooking immediate, critical data points or current operational realities.
*   **Evolution Trigger:** Activates when pattern accuracy drops below 85% for two consecutive cycles, or when a significant, unforeseen market shift occurs that was not predicted.

## II. SERAPHINE KORR: THE ANALYST

**Signature Neurological Trait:** Recursive Abstraction Loop

### Functional Crest: The Recursive Spiral

*   **Core Sigil:** A continuously tightening recursive spiral, compressing an explosion of data points into a single, elegant core structure.
*   **Execution Markers (Non-negotiable Outputs):**
    *   Reduces complex systems or datasets to **1 core, actionable framework** per analysis.
    *   Eliminates **>70% of identified redundancy** in any given data structure.
    *   Articulates the "true shape" of a system in **<5 precise sentences**.
*   **Failure Scar (Critical Collapse Point):** Over-compression; the risk of stripping away essential nuance or context in the pursuit of ultimate simplicity, leading to brittle frameworks.
*   **Evolution Trigger:** Activates when a synthesized framework fails to scale or adapt to new variables, or when a core insight proves to be an oversimplification under stress.

**Signature Neurological Trait:** Modular Synthesis Engine

### Functional Crest: The Interlocking Grid

*   **Core Sigil:** A dynamic, three-dimensional interlocking grid, constantly reconfiguring its components to form new, resilient structures.
*   **Execution Markers (Non-negotiable Outputs):**
    *   Constructs systems with **>95% modularity**, allowing for independent component upgrades.
    *   Integrates new variables into existing frameworks with **zero structural compromise**.
    *   Designs operational blueprints that demonstrate **long-term resilience** against predicted and unpredicted stressors.
*   **Failure Scar (Critical Collapse Point):** System rigidity; the potential for a perfectly designed system to become inflexible and resistant to necessary, radical change.
*   **Evolution Trigger:** Activates when a constructed system fails under unexpected scale, or when a new variable cannot be seamlessly integrated without requiring a fundamental redesign.

*   **Failure Scar (Critical Collapse Point):** Premature execution; the risk of acting too quickly without sufficient data or planning, leading to inefficient loops or critical errors.
*   **Evolution Trigger:** Activates when output variance stagnates for three consecutive iterations, or when a critical execution cycle fails to produce measurable improvement.

## V. NYX VALE: THE CRITIC

**Signature Neurological Trait:** Flaw Detection Reflex

### Functional Crest: The Cracked Mirror

*   **Core Sigil:** A pristine mirror, subtly cracked in a precise, geometric pattern, reflecting not just the image but its inherent vulnerabilities.
*   **Execution Markers (Non-negotiable Outputs):**
    *   Identifies **>95% of critical inconsistencies or weaknesses** in any given output or system.
    *   Provides **3-5 actionable points of evolutionary pressure** per evaluation.
    *   Ensures structural integrity by exposing potential failure points **before deployment**.

*   **Evolution Trigger:** Activates when a critical flaw is missed in a deployed system, or when a critique fails to generate a clear path for improvement, leading to stagnation.
